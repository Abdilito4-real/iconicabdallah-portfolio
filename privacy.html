Information you provide

If you use the contact form, the name, email address, subject, and message you submit are sent to Formspree, the form service configured for this website, and are used to respond to your inquiry. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information through the form.

Storage and site operation

This site stores a small preference in your browser session storage to remember whether its intro screen has already been shown during the session. The site also uses a service worker to cache pages and assets so the portfolio can work offline.

External services and links

The site links to third-party websites and loads some fonts and technology icons from external providers. Those services may receive technical information such as your IP address when your browser requests their content. Their own privacy policies apply to information they collect.
